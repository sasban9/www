Onboarding timeline

This is not a quiz.
It's a slow revelation.

The biggest thing wrong with career tests is that they assume 15 minutes of self-report can outsmart months of actual behaviour. We don't believe that.

Week 1
Observe. Don't perform.
You get dropped into the server. No quiz. No form. Just explore — any guild, any channel, any conversation. The AI watches quietly. This week is deliberately unstructured.
No pressureOpen explorationPassive observation begins
Week 2
First experiment. Low stakes.
Your mentor suggests one micro-project (2-3 hours, your own pace). Not to assess you — to give you something concrete to react to. Your reaction is the data point.
First mentor touchpointOne micro-projectReaction tracked
Week 3–4
Build something inside a guild.
You pick a guild that's pulling you and do a real deliverable with peers. It doesn't have to be good. How you work — the iteration pattern, what you get stuck on, what you finish first — is more valuable than the output.
Peer collaborationProcess > outputFirst archetype signal emerges
Month 2
Pattern detection begins.
Enough data now. The AI surfaces three possible archetypes with confidence scores. Your mentor reviews them. You sit with it — agree, push back, ignore it. All reactions are valid.
First Curiosity Graph draftArchetype confidence scoresMentor validation session
Month 3 onwards
Confidence grows. The map sharpens.
Every week adds resolution. The Curiosity Graph doesn't expire. By Month 6 it knows you better than a year of formal schooling. By Month 12 it's an asset that follows you into college admissions, career conversations, and beyond.
