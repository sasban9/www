The Four-Year Framework

One grade at a time,
the picture gets clearer.

9

Foundation Year

Discover & Explore

Cast a wide net. Track every club, hobby, elective, and weekend activity without judgment. Build the raw data.

10

Pattern Year

Notice & Reflect

What keeps showing up? Identify recurring themes. Start separating things they do because they must vs. because they want to.

11

Depth Year

Test & Validate

Go deeper into 2–3 areas. Shadow professionals, take online projects, document strengths and weaknesses with evidence.

12

Decision Year

Commit with Confidence

By now you have 3 years of evidence. Career choices stop feeling like guesses — they feel inevitable.
